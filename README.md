# CATL Quantitative Equity Research

This repository reconstructs a 2025 summer UBS-style quantitative equity-research project around CATL. CATL is the mentor-assigned focal company, not an ex-post screening winner. The research clock stops at **29 August 2025**.

Every reported result is either generated by code here or tied to a dated filing record. Unsupported figures from earlier resume drafts are intentionally excluded.

## Bottom line

The evidence is positive on CATL's relative operating quality but insufficient for an unconditional stock rating without point-in-time valuation and consensus revisions.

- 2024 battery volume increased, but falling ASPs drove the EV and ESS revenue contraction. The symmetric bridge attributes EV revenue change to +RMB46.5bn from volume and -RMB80.7bn from ASP; ESS to +RMB17.9bn and -RMB20.3bn, respectively.
- CATL's 2025H1 revenue grew 7.27%. EV revenue grew 16.8% while segment gross margin declined 1.07ppt; ESS revenue declined 1.47% while margin improved 1.11ppt.
- The full weekly return model has 61.8% adjusted R-squared and improves out-of-sample squared error by 20.0% versus market-only. During the internship window, common market, peer, and lithium states contributed positively, while the model residual was negative.
- CATL ranks 1/6 on the 2024 peer operating score, but forward Rank IC is unstable and mostly negative. The score is not presented as a validated alpha factor.
- Commodity downside is conditional on how lithium moves map into total direct-material cost and how much is passed through to customers. The repository never treats those scenario assumptions as causal estimates.

Read the complete [research report](reports/generated/research_report.md), [methodology](docs/methodology.md), and [interview defense](docs/interview_defense.md).

## Research structure

1. **Earnings drivers:** filing-based EV/ESS volume and ASP bridge, material-cost calibration, and 2025H1 checkpoint.
2. **Equity attribution:** CATL weekly returns explained by CSI 300, an equal-weighted battery-peer factor excluding CATL, and GFEX lithium carbonate.
3. **Relative positioning:** point-in-time six-company operating score and subsequent-return validation.
4. **Risk:** diagnostic-selected lithium simulation and explicit cost/pass-through sensitivity grid.

## Point-in-time controls

Market observations and filing notice dates after 2025-08-29 cause the build to fail. Annual peer metrics enter only after their public notice dates. CATL's 2025 interim report was released on 31 July 2025, so it is a valid in-internship checkpoint.

## Reproduce

Python 3.11 or newer is required.

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/python -m pip install -e '.[dev]'
make all
```

`make all` downloads public market/fundamental data, builds the point-in-time panels, runs the analysis, regenerates the report, and executes tests. To download the five official CATL PDFs and verify their recorded SHA-256 hashes:

```bash
make filings
```

Raw vendor files and large reproducible Parquet panels are intentionally not committed. Source providers, URLs, retrieval metadata, per-symbol fallbacks, and filing checksums are retained in `data/source_manifest.json` and `data/manual/filing_sources.csv`.

## Data upgrades

The public baseline uses Eastmoney/Sina adapters and official CATL filings. Institutional exports can be placed in the ignored `data/external/` directory using the schemas in [data/README.md](data/README.md). The highest-value upgrades are:

- Wind/Bloomberg point-in-time total returns, valuation multiples, and consensus revisions;
- SMM/Fastmarkets battery-grade lithium spot history;
- dated monthly battery installations, CATL share, and ESS shipment data.

These upgrades would support a valuation-aware investment rating. They are not required to reproduce the transparent public-data baseline.
